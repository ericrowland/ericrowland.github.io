Eric Rowland

Hi! I'm a professor in the Department of Mathematics at Hofstra University.

On my YouTube channel, I make videos about surprising discoveries in mathematics. You can also watch some of my research talks.

I'm writing a book, The Hierarchy of Integer Sequences, for an undergraduate course in combinatorics.

My research is broadly in number theory and combinatorics. I make extensive use of software for conducting experiments, discovering conjectures, and proving theorems. You can read more about my research here.

I'm a big proponent of communicating mathematics as clearly and accessibly as possible. I've collected some advice on writing and refereeing.
